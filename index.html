SERVICES:


  • Corporate Services offered in downtown Toronto
  • For corporate teams of 2-15 we offer onsite tune-ups & repairs. Please include current photos of any bikes in need of specific repair. /li>
  • For corporate teams of 15+we offer onsite tune-ups. Repair inquiries can be scheduled on site - repairs can be scheduled within a 24 hr turn-around.
  • All services are performed by certified and experienced bicycle mechanics.

TUNE-UPS:


All tune-ups include:

  • Brake & Gear adjustment, front and rear
  • Check all bearings (BB, headset & hubs)
  • Minor wheel truing
  • Inflate tires to proper pressure
  • Frame and wheel wipe down
  • Chain, pulley and pivot lubrication
  • Tightening of loose nuts and bolts

PRICING:


  • After your booking form submission, you will receive a quote based on number of bikes for services & estimated hours of work.
  • Cost will be quoted after your form submission and based on the number of bikes, and requested repairs.
  • There is an additional $25 travel fee for locations outside of downton service area.

REPAIRS:


  • We will accommodate onsite reapir requests for groups under 15.
  • We ask that you provide a current photo of the bikes condition and a detailed explanation of the issue.
  • There are no service-packages. Our team will diagnose things that are deemed in the best interest of the Bicycle.
